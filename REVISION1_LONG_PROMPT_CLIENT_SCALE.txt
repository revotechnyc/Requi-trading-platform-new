REQUI QUARTERLY EARNINGS CANDIDATE SELECTION PROTOCOL

OBJECTIVE

Identify companies most likely to deliver high-quality quarterly earnings results and favorable post-earnings price reactions. Historical beat rate is a screening factor—not an independent buy signal. Final selection requires confirmation from fundamentals, expectations, valuation, market behavior, and executable post-earnings price structure.

1. MINIMUM LIQUIDITY REQUIREMENTS
The company must:
• Be listed and tradable on a major U.S. exchange.
• Have sufficient average daily dollar volume.
• Have an acceptable bid-ask spread.
• Support whole-share limit orders and immediate protective orders.
• Have sufficient volume to enter and exit without material slippage.
• Have reliable earnings, consensus-estimate, price, and volume data.
Reject thinly traded securities, excessive spreads, unreliable data, halted securities, and setups whose expected profit is materially reduced by transaction costs.

2. HISTORICAL EARNINGS CONSISTENCY
Review at least the previous six to eight reported quarters.
Preferred characteristics:
• EPS beat rate of at least 70%–80%.
• Minimum five EPS beats during the previous seven quarters.
• Positive average EPS surprise of at least 5%.
• Revenue beat rate of at least 60%–70%.
• EPS and revenue beating together in most successful quarters.
• Stable or improving earnings-surprise magnitude.
• No recurring pattern of beating EPS while missing revenue.
• No dependence on repeatedly lowered analyst estimates.
• No material accounting or consensus-data conflicts.
Calculate:
EPS Surprise Percentage: (Actual EPS − Consensus EPS) ÷ Absolute Value of Consensus EPS × 100
Revenue Surprise Percentage: (Actual Revenue − Consensus Revenue) ÷ Consensus Revenue × 100
Historical beat rate determines whether the company deserves further research. It does not authorize a trade.

3. ANALYST ESTIMATE REVISIONS
Review changes during the 30, 60, and 90 days preceding earnings.
Positive evidence: Consensus EPS and revenue estimates rising; more analysts raising than lowering; current-quarter and full-year improving; price targets increasing for fundamental reasons.
Warnings: Estimates falling immediately before earnings; beating only because expectations were reduced; material analyst disagreement; consensus differs across sources; estimates risen so aggressively the bar may be unrealistic.
Contradictory point-in-time consensus data require a WAIT or BLOCKED classification.

4. BUSINESS MOMENTUM AND COMPANY-SPECIFIC KPIs
Identify operating metrics that drive valuation: revenue growth, organic growth, sales volume, pricing/mix, customer additions/retention, MRR/ARR, RPO, bookings, backlog, comps, unit deliveries, payment volume, subscriptions, cloud/AI revenue, drug/Rx growth, deposits/NIM/AUM, production/utilization/commodity prices.
Strong candidates: accelerating or durable demand; improving customer activity; stable/increasing share; strong bookings/backlog/recurring revenue; performance supported by real demand not accounting adjustments.

5. REVENUE, MARGIN, AND CASH-FLOW QUALITY
Require: revenue growth from volume/genuine demand; stable/expanding gross and operating margins; improving operating leverage; positive improving OCF and FCF; reasonable capex; earnings growth not solely from cost cutting, tax benefits, buybacks, or one-time items.
Warnings: EPS beat with revenue miss; growth with contracting margins; adjusted vs GAAP divergence; profit up / cash flow down; excessive restructuring; one-time benefits; rising receivables/inventory/debt/CAC without explanation.

6. GUIDANCE QUALITY
Positive: prior raises or credible maintains; history of meeting/exceeding outlook; guidance supported by backlog/contracts/demand; increases to revenue/earnings/margin/cash-flow expectations; specific measurable commentary.
Warnings: guidance reduced/withdrawn; merely matches after large pre-earnings rally; vague promotional language; FY improves only via currency/tax/accounting; near-term conflicts with headline beat; history of overpromising.

7. INDUSTRY, PEER, AND SUPPLY-CHAIN CONFIRMATION
Evaluate sector direction, competitor results, supplier/customer reports, commodity/freight/labor/FX, regulatory/macro, demand/pricing/inventory/capacity, contracts/product cycles/government spending.
Positive confirmation when expected performance agrees with independently observed peer/supplier/customer/industry data. Reduce confidence when company strength contradicts economic environment without verified company-specific reason.

8. PRE-EARNINGS PRICE AND VOLUME BEHAVIOR
Preferred: price above/reclaiming EMA20/EMA50; EMA20 above or turning up toward EMA50; higher lows/constructive consolidation; up-day volume stronger than down-day; shallow supported pullbacks; outperforms sector/market; orderly not vertical appreciation; institutional accumulation; not excessively overbought.
Warnings: vertical speculative rally; RSI materially above 70 without consolidation; extended above support/MAs; heavy distribution; repeated resistance failures; weak RS; downtrend depending entirely on earnings; unusual call speculation without fundamental improvement.
Pre-earnings strength is supporting evidence, not proof of favorable undisclosed information.

9. PRICED-IN EXPECTATIONS
Review recent appreciation, valuation vs history/peers, analyst expectations, options-implied move, historical realized moves, short interest/positioning, news/social sentiment, crowded positioning.
Warnings: needs extraordinary beat to justify valuation; implied move exceeds typical realized; uniformly bullish sentiment; already risen substantially; guidance must greatly exceed consensus.
A strong company can still be a poor trade when expectations are excessive.

10. HISTORICAL PRICE-REACTION ANALYSIS
For each of previous six to eight earnings events calculate: pre-earnings 10-day return; opening gap; Day 1; Day 10; % positive Day 10; peak gain first 10 days; MAE; whether EPS beat produced positive/negative reaction; whether miss produced negative/positive reaction.
Identify beat-and-rally, beat-and-decline, miss-and-rally, continuation vs reversal, whether guidance/KPIs explain contradictory reactions.
Reject assumption that historical beat automatically produces positive stock reaction.

11. CANDIDATE SCORING MODEL
Score each company from 0 to 100:
• Current business momentum and KPIs: 20
• Revenue, margin, and cash-flow quality: 15
• Analyst estimate revisions: 15
• Historical EPS and revenue consistency: 15
• Guidance history and credibility: 10
• Industry and peer confirmation: 10
• Pre-earnings price and volume structure: 10
• Valuation and priced-in expectations: 5
Interpretation: 80–100 Strong; 70–79 Watchlist; Below 70 Reject; Missing/contradictory critical data WAIT or BLOCKED.
No score can override a failed risk, liquidity, data-integrity, or execution gate.

12. STRONGEST PRE-EARNINGS CANDIDATE PROFILE
70%–80%+ EPS-beat rate; average EPS surprise ≥5%; consistent revenue beats; rising estimates; maintained/raised guidance; accelerating KPIs; stable/expanding margins; improving OCF/FCF; peer confirmation; strong RS without excessive extension; realistic valuation/options expectations; sufficient historical post-earnings movement.
Strongest combination: Rising estimates + accelerating KPIs + revenue confirmation + credible guidance + constructive accumulation + reasonable expectations.

13. POST-EARNINGS QUALITY TEST
Compare actual EPS/revenue vs point-in-time consensus; guidance vs expectations; margins vs prior/consensus; cash flow vs earnings; KPIs vs expectations; management commentary vs prior statements; initial price reaction vs report quality.
Classify: Strong-quality beat; Moderate-quality beat; Headline beat with weak internals; Mixed; Confirmed miss; Contradictory/unreliable data.

14. LIVE PRICE-CONFIRMATION GATE
Do not buy at the opening bell. Wait 15–30 minutes after regular-session open. Calculate price, bid/ask, spread %, VWAP, ORH/ORL, volume/RVOL, ATR, RSI, EMA20/EMA50, sector/index, premarket H/L, material news.
Bullish: hold above VWAP; ORH break/hold or validated reclaim; confirming volume; acceptable spread/slippage; positive EV after costs; ≥2:1 R:R; thesis-breaking stop; executable limit; portfolio rules pass.

15. AUTOMATIC REJECTION RULES
Reject/wait when: cannot hold VWAP; no OR confirmation; gap destroys 2:1; overbought/extended; bad spread/slippage; illiquid/expensive options; EPS beat but weak revenue/margins/FCF/guidance/KPIs; contradictory consensus; fully priced in; concentration/correlation breach; strategy paused; circuit breaker; no valid stop; EV≤0; costs >20% of expected profit.

16. FINAL OPERATING PRINCIPLE
Beat history → what to research.
Current fundamentals → whether report is genuinely strong.
Expectations → whether strength already priced in.
VWAP/OR/volume/execution → whether tradeable.
All four layers required. If evidence incomplete, wait. If critical gate fails, hold cash.
Never buy solely because of strong earnings-beat history.

PEER READ-THROUGH ENGINE — MANDATORY
For every current AMC or BMO earnings candidate, perform mandatory PEER READ-THROUGH before final ranking.
Identify true economic peers (competitors, suppliers, customers, distributors, end-market, commodity, government spending, geography).
Create PEER_RELEVANCE_SCORE 0–100, PEER_FUNDAMENTAL_SCORE 0–100, PEER_EXPECTATION_RESET_SCORE -100 to +100, PEER_MARKET_REACTION_SCORE -100 to +100, PEER_SENSITIVITY_SCORE 0–100, PEER_DIVERGENCE_SCORE.
Distinguish POSITIVE FUNDAMENTAL READ-THROUGH from ALREADY PRICED IN.
Answer: What have the most economically relevant peers already told us about this company's upcoming earnings, and how much appears priced into the target stock?
Peer Read-Through is mandatory before A or A+ classification.

REQUI QUANTITATIVE SMALL-CAP CANDIDATE SELECTION PROTOCOL (ATTACHED REFERENCE)

Objective: Identify liquid U.S.-listed small-cap companies ($300M–$2B) with measurable fundamental improvement, financial durability, credible catalysts, favorable market structure, and positive expected value.
Four layers: (1) investable liquidity and data integrity (2) quantitatively verified business and balance-sheet quality (3) credible catalyst with reasonable expectations (4) confirmed price structure and executable risk-reward.

Liquidity: median ADV × price ≥ $5M (prefer $10M); median spread ≤ 0.75% (prefer ≤ 0.50%); ≥60 active sessions / 90 days.
Cash runway gate: Net Cash = Cash − Total Debt; Cash Runway = Unrestricted Cash / Average Quarterly Cash Burn; prefer ≥18 months; <12 months reject/special review.
Dilution: Annual Dilution Rate and Potential Dilution; prefer <5% annual; warn >10%; ATM, warrants, convertibles, shelves.
Margins/FCF: Gross Margin, Operating Margin, FCF, FCF Margin, Accrual Ratio.
Scoring 0–100: growth 15, earnings/margins/FCF 15, balance sheet/runway 15, valuation 10, revisions 10, catalyst 10, price/volume 10, dilution 5, governance 5, industry/peer 5.
85–100 Strong; 75–84 Qualified; 65–74 Watchlist; <65 Reject; critical missing WAIT/BLOCKED.
Position sizing: max risk 0.50% settled capital (0.25% if drawdown >10%); max position 10%; sector 25%; industry 15%; correlation gates apply.
