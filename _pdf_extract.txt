OBJECTIVE 
The next revision should make Requi capable of 
answering general, beginner-level market questions using 
predetermined backend workflows. 
The LLM should NOT determine market conditions from 
its internal knowledge. 
The LLM should NOT require the user to know a ticker. 
The LLM should NOT ask unnecessary questions such as 
which country or exchange the user means when the 
request is clearly about the stock market. 
Instead, the application should classify the user’s intent 
and automatically execute the correct market intelligence 
functions before the LLM generates its response.
